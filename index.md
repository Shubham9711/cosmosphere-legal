# CosmoSphere — Support

**CosmoSphere** (Cosmic Journey) is a free educational app that takes you on interactive journeys through the Big Bang, inflation, the expanding universe, black holes, dark matter and dark energy, and the multiverse.

## Common questions
- **Does it need the internet?** No. All journeys and checked answers work offline. Only typed "Ask why?" questions, encyclopedia summaries, Live Earth images and the map need a connection.
- **Is it free?** Yes — no ads, no accounts, no in-app purchases.
- **How do I reset my progress?** Clear the app's storage in your phone's settings, or reinstall the app.
- **An AI answer looks wrong.** Tap **Report this answer** under it. AI answers can be wrong; answers marked **Checked answer** are pre-written and fact-checked.
- **The 3D looks slow on my phone.** Open Explore → Cosmic Archive and set **Graphics quality** to Low.
- **Why does the map ask for my location?** Only if you tap **My location**. Your position stays on your phone.
